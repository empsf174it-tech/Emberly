Our Provenance & Practice

From Green Sourcing to the Final Micron.

We began as coffee roasters frustrated by equipment tolerances that choked delicate micro-lots. Today, Emberly engineers tools that translate terroir faithfully into the cup.

The Origin Story

The Obsession with Burr Alignment

In 2021, while cupping experimental anaerobic coffees from Huila, Colombia, our cupping team noticed sharp differences between two identical commercial grinders. One cup displayed pristine peach and bergamot; the second was muted, bitter, and muddy.

A dial indicator revealed the truth: a 45-micron axial wobble in the burr carrier was generating microscopic fines that ruined extraction uniformity. Standard consumer gear wasn’t built to the aerospace tolerances required for modern light roasts.

We established the Emberly bench laboratory in Seattle to tear down, rebuild, and re-engineer coffee gear from the carrier shims up. Today, every piece in our shop represents that relentless standard.

Rigorous Bench Protocol

How Every Machine is Qualified

We do not sell gear straight from factory shipping crates. Every unit undergoes multi-point bench calibration before receiving its serial certification seal.

1. Dial Runout Verification

Burr carriers are spun on granite surface plates with Mitutoyo indicators. We correct axial runout to ≤2.5 microns using brass micro-shims before motor burn-in.

2. PID Thermal Logging

Espresso boilers run 100 consecutive 27-second simulated extractions through an automated Scace-2 thermofilter device, logging water stability to within ±0.2°C.

3. Particle Diffraction Scan

Test doses are imaged with optical particle-size analyzers to chart unimodal distribution curves, verifying minimal fines migration below 80 microns.
