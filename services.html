Frequently Asked Questions

Equipment & Extraction Details

Common questions regarding burr seasoning, international voltage, and water mineral tolerances.

How many kilos of coffee are needed to season the 64mm DLC burrs?

Our DLC (Diamond-Like Carbon) burrs are pre-polished at the factory, requiring only 1.5 to 2 kilograms of seasoning coffee before reaching peak unimodal clarity. You can start pulling delicious shots right out of the box.

Do your espresso machines run on standard 110V household outlets?

Yes. The Kallio Artisanal Thermal Profiler utilizes intelligent staged dual-boiler heating algorithms that operate safely on standard 15-amp, 110–120V household receptacles. 220–240V models are also available for European and Australian orders.

What brew water specifications do you recommend to prevent scaling?

We advocate water with a total hardness of 50–85 ppm CaCO3 and an alkalinity buffer of 35–45 ppm (pH 7.0–7.4). Avoid untreated municipal hard tap water to preserve boiler integrity and unlock sweet enzymatic aromatics.
